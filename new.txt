2. First build an astrology knowledge base
This should be the foundation of your prediction engine.

Don't put all your astrology knowledge inside one giant AI prompt. Store the rules in a structured database.
A. Planet knowledge
For each planet, store information such as:
Planet	Core themes
Sun	Authority, leadership, vitality, identity
Moon	Mind, emotions, comfort, perception
Mars	Action, courage, competition, energy
Mercury	Communication, analysis, business, learning
Jupiter	Knowledge, expansion, wisdom, guidance
Venus	Relationships, enjoyment, art, harmony
Saturn	Discipline, responsibility, delays, persistence
Rahu	Ambition, unconventional pursuits, amplification
Ketu	Detachment, introspection, specialization

Each planet should have more than a generic description.

Your database should eventually contain:
- Planet in each sign
- Planet in each house
- Planet in each nakshatra
- Planet in each pada
- Exaltation and debilitation
- Own sign
- Moolatrikona
- Retrogression
- Combustion
- Conjunctions
- Aspects
- Functional benefic/malefic classification by ascendant
B. House knowledge
Create a knowledge base for all 12 houses.
House	Primary interpretation domains
1st	Self, body, personality, life direction
2nd	Wealth, family, speech, possessions
3rd	Effort, courage, communication, siblings
4th	Home, mother, property, emotional foundations
5th	Education, creativity, children, intelligence
6th	Competition, debts, service, obstacles
7th	Marriage, partnership, contracts
8th	Transformation, longevity themes, hidden matters
9th	Dharma, fortune, higher education, mentors
10th	Career, work, reputation, status
11th	Gains, income, networks, ambitions
12th	Expenses, retreat, foreign connections, sleep

These are traditional astrological associations, not scientifically validated forecasts.
C. Sign knowledge
Store:
- 12 Rashis
- Element
- Modality
- Ruler
- Characteristics
- Compatibility with planets
- Dignities
D. Aspect knowledge
Your engine should understand traditional Parashari aspects:
- Every planet's 7th aspect.
- Mars: 4th, 7th, 8th.
- Jupiter: 5th, 7th, 9th.
- Saturn: 3rd, 7th, 10th.
- Rahu and Ketu: define the aspect convention explicitly in your system because traditions differ.
Don't mix different astrology traditions without identifying the ruleset.

3. Build the interpretation engine
This is the most important coding part.

Suppose your existing chart calculator returns:
{
  "lagna": "Aries",
  "planets": {
    "Sun": {
      "sign": "Leo",
      "house": 5,
      "degree": 14.25
    },
    "Moon": {
      "sign": "Taurus",
      "house": 2,
      "degree": 18.5
    },
    "Mars": {
      "sign": "Cancer",
      "house": 4,
      "degree": 10.2
    }
  }
}


This is an illustrative example, not Sagar Ghosh's actual birth chart.

Your prediction engine shouldn't immediately send this JSON to an AI and ask, "Predict the life."

Instead, create a pipeline.
Step 1 — Analyze every planet
For every planet, your system should calculate a structured analysis.

Example:
{
  "planet": "Mars",
  "sign": "Cancer",
  "house": 4,
  "dignity": "Debilitated",
  "house_lordship": [1, 8],
  "conjunctions": [],
  "aspects_received": [],
  "aspects_given": [],
  "retrograde": false,
  "combust": false,
  "strength_score": null,
  "interpretation_factors": []
}


Your engine should evaluate:
1. What house does the planet occupy?
2. What sign does it occupy?
3. What houses does it rule?
4. What is its dignity?
5. What planets are conjunct it?
6. What planets aspect it?
7. What does it aspect?
8. Is it retrograde?
9. Is it combust?
10. What is its calculated strength?
11. Is it connected to relevant yogas?
This analysis becomes your planetary interpretation output.

4. Build a scoring and evidence system
Don't generate predictions from a single planetary placement.

Combine multiple indicators.

For example, for career analysis, your engine might examine:
- 10th house
- 10th house lord
- Planets in 10th house
- Aspects to the 10th house
- 2nd house (income)
- 6th house (work and service)
- 11th house (gains)
- Lagna and lagna lord
- Relevant yogas
- Dasha and Antardasha
- D10 divisional chart, if your system supports it
Example of your internal evidence structure
Career evidence analysis

Illustrative structure — not an actual reading

Positive indicators
- 10th lord connected to 11th house
- Jupiter influences the career house
- Relevant career yoga detected
Challenging indicators
- Saturn influencing career significators
- 10th lord placed in a challenging dignity
Timing indicators
- Current Antardasha lord connected to the 10th house
- Relevant transit influence detected
Confidence / evidence quality
- Multiple independent indicators
- Contradictory indicators retained
- Birth-time-sensitive conclusions flagged

You can create a scoring framework for your internal ranking, for example:
Evidence	Example weight
Relevant house lord connection	+3
Relevant planet in target house	+2
Beneficial aspect	+2
Relevant yoga	+2
Challenging aspect	-2
Weak dignity	-1 or -2
Dasha activation	Separate timing score

These are suggested engineering weights, not classical Jyotish rules. You'll need to calibrate them against your chosen texts and interpretation methodology.

Your engine should retain the underlying evidence instead of just outputting a score.

5. Build separate prediction modules
Don't make one universal prediction function. Make independent domain modules.










Career prediction

10th, 6th, 2nd, 11th houses, relevant lords, D10, yogas and timing.













Marriage prediction

7th, 2nd, 5th, 8th houses, Venus, Jupiter where relevant, D9, dasha.













Financial prediction

2nd, 11th, 5th, 9th, 10th houses, wealth yogas and timing.













Education prediction

4th, 5th, 9th houses, Mercury, Jupiter, D24 if supported.













Foreign travel / relocation

9th, 12th, 7th houses, relevant lords, dasha and transit.













Personality analysis

Lagna, Lagna lord, Moon, Sun, planetary influences and dignity.



Each module should have its own rules, evidence gathering, contradictory evidence handling, timing analysis, and report generation.

6. Build the timing engine
Your system already calculates the birth chart. The next step is to use your existing dasha calculations to make time-oriented interpretations.

For example:
Current Mahadasha
       ↓
Current Antardasha
       ↓
Relevant planet's natal position
       ↓
House lordship
       ↓
Relevant houses
       ↓
Yoga involvement
       ↓
Transits
       ↓
Prediction for the selected period


Example
Suppose the system detects:
- Mahadasha lord connected to the 10th house
- Antardasha lord connected to the 11th house
- Both planets connected with career or gains in your defined rules
- Current transits also support your defined career indicators
Your system could generate an interpretation that this period is traditionally associated with increased focus on career development, professional networking or income-related opportunities.

But it should also consider contradictory indicators and avoid saying that a job is guaranteed.

Important: The timing engine should not independently recalculate your chart or dasha. It must use the data your existing calculation engine already produced. The interpretation layer should never modify calculated planetary positions. 
Kundali AI+1


7. Use AI as the interpretation layer
Now you can integrate an LLM.

Your system sends the AI structured analysis, not just raw birth-chart data.
Example AI input
{
  "prediction_category": "career",

    "lagna": "Aries",
